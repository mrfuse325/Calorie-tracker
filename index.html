Macro split (% of calories)

Start with 20% protein, 50% carbs, and 30% fat, or choose your own split within adult reference ranges. Percentages must total 100%.

How the estimate works

Mifflin–St Jeor resting energy: 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age + sex coefficient. Multiply by your selected activity factor for estimated maintenance calories. Activity factors are approximate. Protein/carbs use 4 kcal/g; fat uses 9 kcal/g. Macro targets follow your chosen split, not a uniquely prescribed diet.

Adult reference ranges: protein 10–35%, carbs 45–65%, fat 20–35% of calories. The protein reference of 0.8 g/kg is for generally healthy adults, not an athletic-performance target. Individual needs vary; review estimates with a qualified professional if needed.
