Calculate daily targets

Choose how much weight you want to lose per week to estimate daily calories and macros. General estimates for adults ages 19–80; not designed for pregnancy, breastfeeding, or medical nutrition needs.

Macro split (% of calories)

Start with 20% protein, 50% carbs, and 30% fat, or choose your own split within adult reference ranges. Percentages must total 100%.

How the estimate works

Mifflin–St Jeor resting energy: 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age + sex coefficient. Multiply by your selected activity factor for estimated maintenance calories. Subtract 250, 500, or 1,000 kcal/day for the approximate 0.5, 1, or 2 lb/week options. This uses the reference calculator’s simple 3,500 kcal/lb convention; actual weight change varies and these are not guaranteed loss rates.

Macro targets use your selected calorie target and split, with 4 kcal/g for protein/carbs and 9 kcal/g for fat. Automatic weight-loss targets below 1,500 kcal/day for the male coefficient or 1,200 for the female coefficient, or for an underweight BMI, require professional review. These app limits are not a guarantee of suitability for everyone.

Adult reference ranges: protein 10–35%, carbs 45–65%, fat 20–35% of calories. The protein reference of 0.8 g/kg is for generally healthy adults. Individual needs vary.
